Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The short version: Snuggle Stories does not collect, sell or share any personal information, from children or from adults. Stories are created on your device, there are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. If a grown-up chooses to record themselves reading a story, that recording is saved only on your device and is never uploaded.

This policy covers the Snuggle Stories app (the web app at ssgswole.github.io/snuggle-stories and any iPhone, iPad or Android versions) and this website. Snuggle Stories is designed for children and their families, and we built it so there is simply nothing to collect.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data. There are no sign-ups or accounts. Anything the app saves (your story choices and any grown-up voice recordings) stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

The hero's name and story choices

When you type a hero name or pick a pronoun, age, world, mood or length, that information is used on your device to build the story. It is never sent to us or anyone else. The app may remember your last choices in your device's local storage so they're ready next time. That data stays on your device, and you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's or browser's site data, or by deleting the app.

Read-aloud voice

Stories are read aloud using the text-to-speech voice built into your device or browser. Depending on your device and settings, your operating system or browser maker may provide some voices through its own online services (for example, some "Google" or "Online (Natural)" voices); their own privacy policies apply to that. The app picks an on-device voice whenever one is available.

Storyteller voice (optional). A grown-up can choose to download a natural "storyteller voice" (after a grown-up check). This downloads the voice files (about 47 MB) once from this website (ssgswole.github.io) and keeps them on your device. After that, the voice is generated entirely on your device, even offline. Story text, names and audio are never sent anywhere. Downloading files from our website is an ordinary web request and is not used to identify or track you; we receive no information about your stories. A grown-up can remove the downloaded voice at any time in the app's grown-up settings.

Microphone and your recordings

The full version includes "Record my voice", so a grown-up can record themselves reading a story for their child. The app uses the microphone only when a grown-up opens this feature (after a grown-up check) and taps Start recording. Your device asks for permission first, and the microphone is turned off as soon as the recording screen closes. The app never listens or records at any other time.

  • Stored on your device only. Each recording is saved in the app's own storage on your device, together with the story text, title, hero name, date and length, so your child can replay it with the words highlighted.
  • Never uploaded. Recordings are not sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. There is no cloud backup or sync, and we cannot see, hear or recover them.
  • You're in control. A grown-up can rename or delete recordings at any time under "My recordings" (behind the grown-up check). Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, deletes all recordings.
  • Recordings are meant to be made by grown-ups. Please don't record children's voices.

Apple Personal Voice (iPhone and iPad app)

On iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later), a grown-up can let the app read stories in the Personal Voice they created in the device's Settings (Settings › Accessibility › Personal Voice). The app only asks after a grown-up check, and only if "Allow Apps to Request to Use" is turned on. Apple's software creates the speech on your device. Snuggle Stories does not record, save or upload Personal Voice audio, and it has no access to the recordings used to create your Personal Voice. You can turn off access at any time in Settings › Accessibility › Personal Voice.

Offline use

The app saves its own files on your device so stories work without internet. Grown-up voice recordings (see above) are also kept on your device and work offline.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Snuggle Stories shows no advertising and contains no analytics, tracking pixels, social media plugins or third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data. This website uses no cookies.

Purchases

The free version includes 2 story worlds (Bedtime and Animals). "Unlock all worlds" is an optional one-time purchase of $4.99 (USD; local prices may vary). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their terms and privacy policies. We never see or store your payment details. A grown-up check appears before any purchase or link that leaves the app.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13, or from anyone. Because nothing is collected, there is no personal information for us to review, share or delete. Parents and guardians remain in control: anything saved on the device, including grown-up voice recordings, can be removed in the app's "My recordings" screen, by clearing site data, or by deleting the app. If you ever believe information about your child has reached us, contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Hosting

This website and the web app are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) when pages are loaded, for security and operations. We do not receive or use these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. We will never start collecting children's personal information without first updating this policy and obtaining verifiable parental consent where the law requires it.
