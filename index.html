    Tap Listen and the story is read aloud, highlighting the part being read so early readers can follow along.

Put it on your home screen

Snuggle Stories installs like an app straight from your browser. It gets its own icon and opens full-screen.

📱 iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

🤖 Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Questions parents ask

What ages is it for?

Ages 3 to 11. Pick 3–5, 6–8 or 9–11 and the words and story length adjust to fit.

Are the stories safe for young kids?

Yes. Every story is built from hand-written, gentle plots: kind friends, small problems, and calm, happy endings. Nothing is pulled from the internet and there are no chat features.

Do you collect my child's name or any data?

No. Stories are created right on your device. We don't collect, store or send names, stories or usage data, and there are no ads, analytics or accounts. See our privacy policy.

Does it need internet?

Only the very first time you open it. After that, stories work offline. The read-aloud voice comes from your phone or computer, so voices vary a little between devices.

What do I get for $4.99?

All 8 worlds and all 56 adventures, plus longer stories, unlocked once, with no subscription. The free version keeps Bedtime and Animals with unlimited stories.

Can my child buy things by accident?

No. A grown-up check appears before any purchase or outside link.

Is there an App Store or Google Play version?

They're coming soon. Until then, you can add the web app to your home screen (see the steps above) and it works just like an app.
